Your password manager is only as strong as its Master Key.

Even if DinoVault uses military-grade, local-first authenticated encryption... if your master password is dinosaur123, it won't save you.

Pro-Tip: Use a "Passphrase" instead of a password. (e.g., "Coffee-Keyboard-Rainstorm-42!"). It is mathematically harder for a computer to crack, and much easier for your human brain to remember.
